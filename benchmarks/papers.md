## Notes

1. M83F2JH9 ("Deep Learning for Portfolio Optimization", Zhang-Zohren-Roberts 2020) is a junk parent with 13 attachments, among them three roadmap PDFs that have no item of their own: FO 2011 SINUM (RAQ36U5I, HXPMKT9H), Chen-Wan-Lin 2018 (3XWEWWT6, 9WII8MMM) and Dean-Glowinski ETNA 2006 (8583MBXV, 4JT45LX9).
   JC should create proper parent items and re-parent these attachments; this check did not change the library.
2. Author mismatch: the semi-Lagrangian mixed-FD paper is by Chen, Wan and Lin (J. Sci. Comput. 76, 2018) according to its title page, but the roadmap and the survey call it "Chen-Wan-Wang 2018".
   Neither side was corrected here.
3. Metadata gaps: U7QZ6BDY has no year or journal (Math. Comp. 80, 2011); HHWECHN6 has a truncated title and no year (Numer. Math. 54, 1988); 26H2AHTT says 2018 for Math. Comp. 88(316), which is the 2019 issue; GV9DAREQ is the arXiv v2, not the Eng. Comput. journal version.
4. Missing from Zotero: Nystrom 2023, Hu et al. 2026 (C-PINN and KAN), Caboussat et al. 2026 (Deep Ritz splitting), Shortley-Weller 1938, Amos et al. 2017 (ICNN), Feng-Neilan 2009 (mixed FE, vanishing moment), Froese 2018 meshfree.
   The survey read the four neural papers from arXiv, so their table and T-id mappings above come from the survey, not from a Zotero PDF.
   The Nystrom 2023 row was checked against the arXiv v3 PDF for #12, which corrected which method its tables belong to.
5. The 19-point 3D monotone scheme is not a separate paper: it is FO 2011 SINUM Table 4; FO 2011 JCP Table 4 gives the 3D hybrid results on the same three problems.
6. Figure-only sources (no numbered tables): Feng-Jensen 2017 and BCM 2016 (MA-LBR); their best reproduction level under ADR 0002 is "figure only".
